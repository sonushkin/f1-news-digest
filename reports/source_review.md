- Format: RSS XML
- Account or API key: not required

The feed URL is listed by current RSS directories and was reported as working
on the review date. BBC's developer documentation identifies its sports feeds
as RSS 2.0 and states that feed data is governed by BBC feed terms. A live feed
is not guaranteed to remain available, so failure handling is part of the
program requirements.

## Attribution and use

BBC's terms require clear source credit and prohibit misleading presentation,
removal of attribution, and unauthorized commercial use. The project will:

- identify every item as coming from BBC Sport;
- keep the original article link;
- avoid BBC logos and any suggestion of endorsement;
- remain a non-commercial academic project;
- avoid committing or redistributing downloaded feed content;
- avoid modifying or presenting the feed description as AI-generated text.

For commercial use or redistribution, the user must review the current BBC
terms and obtain any permission or licence that may be required.
